# FlyLab model and evidence labeling

- Distinguish real MaleCNS anatomy and connectivity from modeled neural activity.
  Simplified rate dynamics are connectivity-informed, not biologically validated.
- Label learned readouts, engineered visual inputs and motor interfaces separately.
  Do not describe controller output as a fly's desires, consciousness or general
  programming ability.
- Display neural events from recorded simulation timestamps. Do not invent spikes,
  propagation paths, synapse positions or missing neuron coordinates.
- Keep native Chromium key events, physical contact acknowledgements, source
  changes and rendered results traceable to their recorded session.
- Separate simulation time from elapsed wall time. Report measured performance
  with its hardware, model settings and trial scope.
- Preserve provenance and distinguish held-out evaluation from training results.
  A successful single-heading trial does not establish general webpage synthesis.
- The body, visual interface and neural dynamics are different modeling layers,
  not an exact reconstruction of one biological fly.
